# Private Beta: Onboarding Guide (for invited testers)

**Not sent to anyone yet.** Invitations need the founder's go-ahead (see `FINAL_PRIVATE_BETA_GO_NO_GO.md`).

## Welcome
Bagla Finance helps you keep an exact record of your own money, shared group costs and family budgets. This is an **invite-only beta**: features may change, and we may ask you for feedback.

**Please read first:**
- **It's a record-keeping app.** It doesn't connect to your bank or UPI apps, doesn't move money, and doesn't give investment advice.
- **Check important totals against your bank statement.** Totals come from what you and your group enter.
- **Beta data may be reset**, with notice and an export option first (see the Terms).
- **Use a password you don't use anywhere else** (12+ characters).

## First login (5 minutes)
1. Open the link you were sent → **Account & Sync** → **New here? Create an account**. Enter your name, email and password.
2. **Add your first expense:** Dashboard → **+ Expense** → amount, shop, category → **Save Expense**. Any form can be closed with **X**, **Esc** or the phone's **Back** button; if you've typed something, you'll be asked before anything is discarded.
3. **Set a budget:** Budgets → **+ Create Budget**.
4. **Start a group** (flatmates, a trip): Groups → **Create Your First Group** → name, type, people → **Create group**. Add an expense and choose **Equally**, **Exact amounts**, **Percentages** or **Shares**; the list shows each person's share before you save.
5. **Invite someone to your group:** Members → **Invite to join**, then send them the code yourself. We don't send messages.
6. **Family space (optional):** Family → start one and invite members with a code. **Your personal entries stay private** unless you choose to share them.

## What to expect
| Feature | Status in this beta |
|---|---|
| Entries, budgets, goals, groups, family, recurring bills, statement import (CSV/XLSX), exports | Working |
| AI assistant | Answers from your own records with built-in rules. AI answers only if we switch AI on, and then each answer says so. It never changes your records. |
| Bill scanning | Attach a photo and type the details; nothing is saved until you press Save. Automatic reading is off. |
| Reminders | Inside the app only. No email or SMS. |
| Bank / UPI connection, payments, PDF statements, Hindi/Gujarati | Not available |

## Your data
- **Download everything:** Account & Sync → **Download (.json)**.
- **Delete your account:** ask us through Support during the beta (a self-service button is coming). Shared group records stay for the other members, with your name removed.
- **Who sees what:**
  - Only you see your entries.
  - Group members see that group's expenses.
  - Family members see only what you share.
  - Bagla staff tools can't see your financial records.

## Support, feedback and bugs
- **Support:** in the app → **Account features → Support** (a ticket), or reply to your invitation email.
- **Feedback:** the in-app feedback form, or a short note any time.
- **Bug reports:** what you did, what you expected, what happened, and the screen. If an error shows a **request ID**, include it; it helps us find the problem without seeing your data.
- **Something looks wrong with money:** stop, take a screenshot, and tell us. Please don't try to "fix" it with balancing entries.
